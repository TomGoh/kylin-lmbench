## Additional findings

- probe3's adjacency is not a valid stand-in for lmbench's own buffers, which undercuts the argument used in nx#3 (though not its conclusion). In expH3.log and expH3-frames.log (396 of 396 lmbench points matched to a layout record) lmbench's own 8 MB buffers had adjacency 0.00 to 0.20 in all 12 runs while probe3 run immediately before and after had 0.59 to 0.99; in expH2.log lmbench's 8 MB reading stayed at 10.04 to 10.46 ns whatever probe3's adjacency was. The direct evidence that lmbench is layout-sensitive is expH4.log (unmodified lat_mem_rd: 8.90 against 10.62 ns at 8 MB, 8.84 against 11.53 to 11.64 ns at 64 MB).
- A second signature of the two slots 0x22c0000000 and 0x2300000000 that no claim mentions: the number of page walks per access. In the random chase at 6 and 8 MB (expV2, expV3, class clear, pages taken evenly) the walk count of the other ten slots rises with the number of 2 MiB regions a page set spans (6 MB: 0.86 to 0.875 at 28 to 58 regions, 0.94 to 0.97 at 223 to 349 regions, r = 0.77; 8 MB: r = 0.93), whereas the two slots, whose sets span 247 to 282 regions, show 0.849 and 0.850 at 6 MB (0.948 expected from the trend) and 0.982 and 0.984 at 8 MB (0.997 expected, 7.6 residual standard deviations low). This is what one would expect if 2 MiB stage-2 entries occupy translation-cache capacity and these two slots need almost none, so it supports the identification of the two slots as larger blocks; it is an observation from the logs, not a test, and the stage-2 state was still never read from EL2.
- The slot effect of nx#8 is not visible at 6 and 8 MB in the random chase although walks occur there (+0.5 and +0.7 ns medians, sign changes between runs). At 6 MB the two slots take 9% fewer walks per access, which offsets a higher cost per walk; at 8 MB the scatter near the L3 capacity is several ns. Exp Z does show the effect at 2048 pages (+3.4 to +3.6 cycles).
- In Exp X1 the random pattern at 8 MB differs between the two loop forms in the PMU counts as well as in time: page walks per hop 0.98 to 0.99 (single-load loop) against 0.92 to 0.93 (unrolled), in each of the three seeds. The pre-registered prediction of less than 1% difference for the random pattern was therefore not met at 8 MB (2.1%), and the worklog's statement that all three predictions held (11.10) is too strong.
- The gap between lmbench and probe3 first noticed between Exp H and Exp R (8.5% to 16.4%) is larger at 12 to 64 MB than the loop-form effect measured in Exp X1 (unrolled / loop 1.09 to 1.10): 4 to 7 percentage points remain, consistent with the probe3 buffers of Exp R having been partly adjacent (0.34 to 0.86) while lmbench's 12 to 16 MB buffers are typically scattered (Exp H3).
- Experiments B and L were not measured with probe3 or frames2 but with probe.c and layout.c (run_all.sh, run2.sh), and Exp F with frames.c. All three contain the same single-load loop statement, so the loop-form caveat applies to them by source inspection, but only probe3 and frames2 were actually run in both forms.
- The device binaries are not in the repository (binaries-n80.txt holds only checksums; device compiler gcc 12.3.0). That the default form compiles to one load per iteration and the unrolled form to 100 consecutive loads was checked only on a local compile of the committed source with gcc 15.2 -O2 (a functional check, not a measurement); the 24 KiB readings of 4.000 cycles per hop in both forms are consistent with it.
- In all shared-against-clear comparisons of this bundle (V2, V3, X2, Z) the shared class comes from a single GiB slot, 0x20c0000000 (two passes per run; in Exp Z at 1024 pages also 0x2080000000), because only that slot holds enough pool pages in VM-shared 2 MiB regions. Comparing with the clear pages of the same slot gives similar extras, so the slot is not the cause, but the evidence is one slot.
- Physically contiguous page sets can show far fewer page walks per access than scattered ones in the random chase (expV3.log, 16 MB, pick=low: 0.81 for the shared set and 0.77 for the clear set of slot 0x22c0000000, against 0.99 for the other clear sets). Comparisons that involve the contiguous pick are therefore not at equal walk counts.
- The ordering 'prediction committed before the run' rests on two unsynchronised clocks (workstation commit time against device log time) with margins of 78 s (Exp X), 14 s (Exp Y), 1 s (Exp V3) and 31 s (Exp Z). The ordering relative to the availability of results is secure in all four cases (the runs lasted 2 to 24 minutes and the result commits came after the runs ended); the prediction texts at those commits are the same as in the current worklog.
- Cross-machine consistency check for the prediction in nx#9, indicative only: on the D80F3 workstation without stage-2 (expN-d80f3.log, probe3 cyc_est) a walk costs 27.99 - 14.58 = 13.4 cycles, so the stage-2 extra on the N80 would be about 11 cycles per walk in the 2 MiB slots and about 15 in the two candidate slots. The N90 Figure 7 extra of 11.7 to 14.6 cycles per access lies between the two and does not tell which state the N90 host map was in.
- Timing label in nx#3: the 16 stride-256 sweeps of Exp H used for the medians ran 18:47:40 to 18:58:22, not 'about 18:50 to 19:15' (that is roughly the whole of run9, 18:44:38 to 19:13:39). Exp R, the source of the adjacency figures, ran 18:35 to 18:40, before Exp H.
