## Additional findings

- The pcp autotuning (decay_pcp_high, high_min/high_max) is in both kernel tags (73.76 for the June N90 data, 79.93 for the N80 data). So the earlier framing in worklog 11.25 and in nn#11/nn#12, a fixed per-CPU list of about 1600 pages, is wrong on both machines, not only on the N80. The analyst's revision in worklog 11.30 gets this right, but its new mechanism has a problem. The claimed mechanism is that the 6 MB child's freed pages hit the shrunken limit and nr_pcp_free bulk-frees the list. Yet in H7 run 1, which started with high at 1781, the 6->8 MB step did not break: the 8 MB child got 98 percent of the 6 MB child's frames back. The break occurred only in runs 2-4, which started with high at 13444-18028. So the break is not tied to the list being at its floor at run start, and the revised mechanism is also untested.
- The workstation (D80F3, no host stage-2) shows a much smaller layout effect at strides 64-256 (8 MB: 1.06-1.11; 16-64 MB: 1.03-1.06) than the N80 under protected mode (1.19-1.33). On the N90, three of four boot modes have no host stage-2, yet all four show the same about-1.2 level ratio. Any argument that matches the N80 layout ratio to the N90 two levels (nn#14, nn#16, nn#17(b)) has to explain this. The analyst's docs mention the size difference (L2: 'about a third'), but the matching claims do not carry it.
- The N90 quiesced low level at 8 MB, stride 256 (10.32 ns) is close to the N80's scattered or natural lmbench value (10.36-10.62 ns), not to its sorted value (8.90 ns). Under E2, the N90's low level would have to be the contiguous state on a platform about 16 percent slower than the N80. That is an extra parameter, and it is not 'no free parameter'.
- In H2, the interconnect readings ('noc0', 'noc', and those in the ## lines) are all taken around the probe3 intervals and at the start and end of each sweep, never during an lmbench sweep. 'Interconnect 1800000 at every reading' is true, but no reading falls in the lmbench measurement windows.
- nn#10's 'prediction (b) failed': the comparison group defined in 11.23 (runs where both probe3 adjacencies are below 0.3) was empty, so (b) was untestable as written. The analyst's conclusion that nothing follows is still the right reading.
- Minor: nn#16 says '7.5 to 9 percent faster' at 3-4 MB, strides 128/256; the data give 0.925 and 0.907, i.e. 7.5 to 9.3 percent. nn#17's '1 of 40 at stride 32' counts a single +8 percent value; at the 1.12 threshold used for the other strides it is 0 of 40.
- Scripts and outputs are in <scratch>/wf5/verify-nn-alloc-adversary/. After forming my verdicts I compared them with wf2-results/verify-nn-alloc-primary.json: same verdict on all five claims (partly-correct), no substantive disagreement. My additional points: the CV of the quotient using D80F3 ratios, the N90 absolute low level matching the N80 scattered state, H7 run 1 as a counter-example to the capacity mechanism, reverse-order recycling at all sizes up to 64 MB, the dependence on shmem_enabled in hugepage_pmd_enabled, and the slight 32 MB drift in H2.
