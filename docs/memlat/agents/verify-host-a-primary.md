## Additional findings

- Framing error in the bundle headline (and in claim host-stats#25, outside this bundle): 'the sign reverses ... in the standalone re-run' is not true for the comparison the paper reports. In the standalone logs pkvm - kvmoff at 8 MB is +0.074 ns (+0.65 %, Welch p = 0.89; medians +2.5 %): near zero, not reversed. The sign is negative only against nvhe (-2.81 %) and vhe (-3.01 %).
- Sequential stride 256, CONFIG.host: pkvm is above kvmoff in all 8 cells from 6 to 64 MB (+1.03, +3.55, +7.64, +2.37, +2.08, +2.60, +0.89, +0.29 %) and above nvhe in 7 of 8 (nominal Welch p < 0.05 at 6, 12, 16, 32 MB), but below vhe in 6 of 8, while vhe is above kvmoff in all 8 (+0.3 to +6.8 %). 'No consistent pkvm effect' is correct only relative to the spread of the three no-stage-2 sessions (pkvm vs their average: +1.2, +1.0, +5.7, +1.2, -0.1, +1.2, -1.5, -0.7 %); a statement that pkvm equals kvmoff in the neighbouring cells would be wrong.
- Data kinds H and D must be kept apart at the LLC boundary: on the N80 (protected mode only, NoC governor 'performance') the 8 MB sequential latency is a unimodal continuum (30 runs, 8.25-9.74 ns, dip test p = 0.84) and a similar contiguity-related spread exists at 6 MB (6.30-7.79) and 12 MB (8.58-10.45). On the N90 the pattern is two discrete values and only in the 8 MB cells (dip p about 0.001 at stride 256; the 6 MB and 12 MB cells are unimodal, p = 0.52 and 0.34). In addition the non-quiesced N90 nvhe session sits on the low value in 40 of 40 measurements at strides 128-1024 (quiesced sessions: 65 of 160 low). The N80 contiguity result therefore does not reproduce the N90 signature and should not be presented as its established cause; a session-level platform state clearly exists on the N90.
- The L3-capacity arithmetic (lines touched = capacity of the reachable sets at 8 MiB) holds for every stride of at least 64 bytes, including stride 64 (131072 lines = 8192 sets x 16 ways), whose 8 MB cell is not two-valued (dip 0.044, p = 0.84; 35 of 40 values in 4.04-4.36 ns, five at 4.55-5.21). The equality is therefore not specific to the two-valued cells.
- lmbench semantics relevant to the onset claims: in the random test the page list is a permutation of ALL pages of the 64 MB buffer (src/lib_mem.c:146 and :152, permutation() at src/lib_timing.c:1632-1654; buffer malloc(maxlen + 2 pages) at lib_mem.c:153), and thrash_initialize uses its first npages entries (lib_mem.c:254-262). At 'size S' the chain therefore visits S/4 KiB pages scattered at random over the whole 64 MB virtual range, one new page per hop. '3 MB (768 pages)' is a page count, not an address span; arguments about TLB reach, walk caches or page-table footprint must use that. The sequential test uses the first S bytes contiguously (lib_mem.c:198-201).
- Second example of a boundary cell with two values: random access at 0.047 and 0.0625 MB (L1d boundary, 64 KiB) alternates between 2.224 ns and 3.9-5.5 ns across iterations in every configuration (0.047 MB: kvmoff 5 fast of 10, nvhe 1, vhe 3, pkvm 3, nvhe-noisy 3), giving median differences of 20-30 % between sessions with no stage-2 involved.
- Provenance limits: (a) the standalone logs carry no timestamps or uptime and scripts/standalone-mem-bench.sh is absent from the repository and its history, so the procedure of that campaign, including 'one boot per configuration' and hence every '8 boot sessions' analysis, is documented only; (b) pkvm try1 raw files are in no commit of any ref (git log --all), confirming that they cannot be analysed; (c) two summary files of earlier 5-iteration runs (kvmoff 2026-06-03T14:17:42, vhe 2026-06-03T15:15:15) have no raw iteration files: the raw reports present belong to the 10-iteration runs (start times match the other summaries).
- Analyses of these random-access data with quartile-based outlier rules or plain means are unreliable: up to 4 of 10 baseline iterations are in the fast state at 16-48 MB (kvmoff 3, 2, 4, 3 flagged at 16, 24, 32, 48 MB), so Tukey fences flag nothing and the mean-based pkvm - kvmoff difference reaches +17.3 ns (+10.9 %) at 16 MB against +7.7 ns by medians, main mode or Hodges-Lehmann.
- 'The same against all three baselines' (headline) holds only to within 1-3 ns: for sizes of 8 MB and more the difference of medians averages 7.37 ns vs kvmoff, 7.34 vs nvhe and 6.31 vs vhe (5.3-5.9 ns at 24-48 MB, where the vhe session is slower).
