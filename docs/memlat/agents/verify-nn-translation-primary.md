## Additional findings

- nn#6 / Exp Z: the walk count quoted for the class 'regions shared with the VM' (0.967) is the value of one row (seed 51, pass 1); the six rows give 0.946-0.973, median 0.952, mean 0.956, and the cost per walk is 34.1 cycles, not 33.6. The same pair (0.967, 33.6) stands in the analyst's worklog table of section 11.21 and in memlat-rootcause.zh-CN.md (table row 'shared with the VM'). This class exists for one slot only (0x83) and carries 0.04-0.06 L2 refills per hop, so its figure is not a pure translation cost.
- N90 precision (results/n90-day3-host CSVs): boots that should not differ in translation differ by more than the claims allow for. Random chase: VHE minus kvm-off is +1.2, +3.0, +2.1, +1.2 ns at 16, 24, 32, 48 MB (nVHE minus kvm-off stays within 0.24 ns). Sequential chase at 16-64 MB: the VHE boot (no stage-2) exceeds kvm-off by MORE than the protected boot does at every stride from 64 up (stride 64: VHE +0.11 to +0.23 ns, protected +0.06 to +0.12; stride 1024: VHE +0.59 to +1.27, protected +0.39 to +0.70), so the sequential data carry no stage-2-specific signal. The protected increment of the random chase is cleanly determined only at 8, 12 and 64 MB (6.3 to 7.3 ns). With means instead of medians it would read 5.9 to 17.3 ns because 1 to 4 of 10 kvm-off iterations at 12-64 MB are in a faster state; the reviewer's +3.25 percent at 64 MB is the ratio of the ten-iteration means (188.13 / 182.21 ns); by medians it is +3.51 percent.
- N90 against the N80 classes: where the N90 increment is clean (8 and 12 MB: 12.4 to 13.1 cycles per access at 0.965 to 1.00 walks) it equals the N80 class 'under 2 MiB blocks' (+11.8 per hop, about 12.3 per walk) and lies about 3 cycles below the class 'can still be 1 GiB blocks' (+15.2 per hop, about 16 per walk), although by the code a host without VMs has its RAM pre-mapped with the largest blocks. Either the N90 buffers were not under 1 GiB blocks or the class costs do not carry over between the machines; the block sizes were read from EL2 on neither machine.
- N90 below 8 MB: the protected increment is 1.3, 3.1 and 9.4 cycles at 3, 4 and 6 MB, while the same access pattern on the N80 (expA.log, ev2d) has 0.18, 0.48 and 0.84 walks per access (per-seed ranges 0.13-0.27, 0.34-0.53, 0.77-0.88); that would be only 5 to 10 cycles per walk at 3-4 MB against 12 to 15 from 8 MB on. A constant cost per walk does not reproduce this; walks were never counted on the N90, so it stays open.
- nn#3: the table values are medians over the three repetitions, not means (means differ by up to 0.12). The repetitions are ordered seed 61 > 67 > 71 in all 14 walk configurations, so the quoted standard errors (0.16 for the slope, 0.59 for the square term) treat a systematic repetition effect as noise; with the repetition as a factor they are 0.05 and 0.15, and the square term (-0.65) is then significant but small, and absent in cycles per walk.
- nn#4: prediction (c) of worklog 11.20 (N80 minus workstation still 14 to 15 cycles at 2048 x 1 after ascending first touch) was not met (12.95) and is not mentioned in the claim. The -1.6 cycles of (b) is a difference of medians; paired by repetition the median change is -1.06, and it is fully accounted for by the lower walk count (-0.04 walks x about 26.5 cycles per walk).
- Drift within a process: at 2048 x 1, and only there, the time per hop falls monotonically over the 11 timed intervals on both machines (N80 -1.5 to -2.0 percent, workstation -2.4 to -3.7 percent; the minimum is among the last three intervals in 52 of 53 workstation processes and 20 of 21 N80 processes), so the configuration used for every cost-per-walk figure had not reached a steady state in the 1.2 s measured. The cross-machine difference is unaffected (+12.9 to +13.0 with minimum, median, mean, first or last interval), the absolute per-walk costs are upper bounds.
- probe3 in tlb mode prints contig_frac=0.0000 and rcontig_frac=0.0000 by construction (probe3.c lines 375-381 count adjacency only in sequential mode), so these fields say nothing about frame contiguity in Exp N2, N4 and N5; l2over is the only layout measure there.
- Provenance: the raw logs used are identical to their committed versions (N80 logs committed at 21:46, 22:05, 22:18, 22:37 right after each run; the workstation logs expN-d80f3.log and expN4-d80f3.log were first committed at 22:54, 70 and 45 minutes after their runs, but their medians already appear in the worklog committed at 21:46 and 22:18). The pre-run predictions precede the device run starts by 14 to 33 seconds on two different clocks. The analyst's worklog and conclusion document were being edited and committed while I verified (commits up to 00:28 on 2026-10-01), so line numbers in those documents are not stable; I cite sections and commits.
- Machines: the N80's SoC is a Phytium D3000M whose cpu0 is a 1.9 GHz, capacity-670 core (two of the eight cores run at 2.9 GHz), the workstation's D3000 has eight 2.5 GHz cores; the MIDR is identical but the reported physical address range differs (IPA limit 40 against 48 bits). 'Same cores' therefore means same core type; the claims list the board and the IPA limit but not the different SoC model.
