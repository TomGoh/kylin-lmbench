## Additional findings

- Exp Z shared-class figure is wrong in the claim and in both documents (worklog line 1078, memlat-rootcause line 453): the six class=shared lines at tlb1 8 MB have walks 0.9456-0.9726 (median 0.952, mean 0.956), not 0.967, and the cost per walk is 34.1 (33.85-34.33 per line), not 33.6. 0.967 equals a single line's value and also the unrelated Exp A figure for 8 MB.
- Exp N3 is cleaner per walk than per hop: cycles per walk = 23.98 + 3.96 (s.e. 0.013) x share with residual s.d. 0.03 cycles and no curvature. The 0.4-0.8 cycle offsets between the three repetitions in cycles per hop are entirely walk-count differences (about 0.97, 0.95, 0.94 walks per hop for seeds 61, 67, 71). The analyst's s.e. of 0.59 for the quadratic term ignores this repetition effect; with it the per-hop curvature is -0.65 (s.e. 0.14).
- Unit mix-up in worklog 11.23 (not in the JSON claim): it cites Exp Z as showing 'about 0.17 per 100 regions in the same direction' in support of the pooled probe3 term of 0.28 per 100 regions. The Exp Z figure is cycles per HOP and comes only from walks per hop rising with the region count (0.0076 per 100 regions, R^2 0.94); in cycles per WALK, the unit of the pooled regression, Exp Z shows -0.013 (s.e. 0.023) over 30-349 regions and N3 +0.012 (0.013). The statement in memlat-rootcause 3.7 that a walk is 'slightly dearer when the pages are spread over more 2 MiB regions (0.2-0.3 cycles per 100)' is therefore not supported by the controlled data; within probe3 runs a region term of 0.25-0.45 per 100 persists after adding the slot-0x83 share, cause unknown.
- The pooled N2+N4+N5 regression omits a significant factor. The share of pages in slot 0x83 (where 66 percent of free pool pages are in VM-shared regions) enters at 6.1 (s.e. 0.8) pooled and 8.7 (s.e. 0.4) in N4 alone (R^2 0.99), and removes most of N4's +1.0 cycle offset. N5's buffers had only 4-10 percent of their pages in that slot, so the correction-table entry 'the guess of 11.21 was wrong' is established for N5's buffers only, not for the N4 runs that prompted it. This analysis is mine, exploratory and post hoc.
- Tension inside the Figure 7 inference: the N90 increment fits 12.4-12.8 cycles per walk, which equals the N80 2 MiB-block class minus the workstation (about 12 per walk) and is 3-4 cycles below the 1 GiB-candidate class minus the workstation (about 16). Yet by the source (host stage-2 prepopulated with 1 GiB blocks, stage-2 code byte-identical at tags 6.6.0-73.76 and 6.6.0-79.93, no VM by procedure) the N90's pages should mostly be under 1 GiB blocks, as memlat-rootcause 3.7 itself assumes. The N80 class model therefore over-predicts the N90 (8.4-9.0 ns against 6.5-8.1 ns), or the workstation baseline is 3-4 cycles too low for the laptops, or the class labels are not what they are taken to be. The data cannot say which.
- The N90 data themselves remove one of the listed cross-machine differences: nVHE (host kernel at EL1, no stage-2) and kvm-off (kernel at EL2) agree within -0.22 to +0.24 ns at every size from 8 to 64 MB, so the exception level of the host kernel does not change the random-access cost on this core. VHE, the same translation regime as kvm-off in another boot, is up to 3.0 ns slower at 24 MB, so boot-to-boot variation at 16-48 MB is 1-3 ns and the protected increment is known to about +-1.5 ns.
- The three stage-2 classes are consistent with the kernel source at the N80 tag but were never observed directly: prepopulation with the largest blocks, 1 GiB blocks permitted, a split block refilled one level down (KVM_PGTABLE_S2_PREFAULT_BLOCK is in KVM_HOST_S2_FLAGS), owner annotations at 4 KiB, merge-back when no counted entry remains. Slots 0x8b and 0x8c are the only whole-RAM slots without VM pages (iomem-n80.txt, guest-regions-*.txt) and the only two that cost about 4 cycles more per walk in Exp Z. The VM region list covers only present pages of the VMM's guest mapping; regions split by hypervisor metadata or host-hyp shared pages are invisible to it.
- 'Same cores' holds for MIDR (0x700f8620 on all cores of both machines) and L1/L2 geometry, which I read on the workstation directly. The IPA limits of 40 and 48 bits are derived from ID_AA64MMFR0_EL1.PARange, so the two chips differ in at least one core configuration parameter; identity of the TLB and walker cannot be established from available sources.
- Prediction bookkeeping: the commits that state the predictions precede the run starts by 18 s (N), 33 s (N2), 23 s and 33 s (N3, workstation N4), 14 s (N5), comparing workstation commit times with device log times; the clocks of the two machines were not compared. The worklog discloses trial runs before the N3 and N4 predictions were written. Each raw log used here has exactly one commit and the working tree is clean.
- Functional check only, not a measurement: probe3.c built from the repository on this workstation prints the logged fields (pfn=na, evXX=na) and gave single-run estimates of 13.97-13.99 for 1024 x 2 and 24.5-24.8 for 2048 x 1, in line with the logged workstation values.
