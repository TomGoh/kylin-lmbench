## Must drop or soften

- **arch-literature#6**: Both verifiers: partly-correct. The min(stage-1, stage-2) size rule is an inference (Arm patent, x86 literature, Cortex-A53 TLB format), not an Arm ARM rule. 'Same TLB reach as native' and 'reach is lost only where stage 2 is finer' are not established for FTC862: stage-2-only and intermediate entries may take TLB capacity (Neoverse N1 L2 TLB; Gandhi et al. saw 1.29-1.62x more misses), and no mode has TLB-miss counts. Safe wording: The Arm ARM (DDI 0487E.a, D5.10.1 and D5.10.2) does not dictate the form in which a TLB stores entries, mentions splintering a page when caching it, and only requires an invalidation to cover at least the size in the translation table entry. It is an inference, not an architectural rule, that one combined VA->PA entry covers at most min(stage-1 size, stage-2 size) (supported by Arm patent US 10,649,907 B2, x86 practice and the Cortex-A53 TLB format). For the pKVM host, 4 KiB stage-1 pages therefore give 4 KiB combined entries whatever the stage-2 block size, i.e. entries no smaller than native. Whether the TLB reach is unchanged is unknown for FTC862. The N90 host data show no sign of lost reach (no pkvm increment at 0.75-1.5 MB, +0.15 to +0.21 ns at 2 MB) but do not prove it.
- **arch-literature#10**: Both verifiers: partly-correct. The arithmetic is right but conditional on premises not verified for the N90 (40-bit IPA known only from boot lines quoted in internal docs, a same-MIDR D80F3 logs 48 bits; 1 GiB blocks under all five addresses of a walk never measured; walk-cache and IPA-cache models are Cortex-A53's). The time conversion used 1.9 GHz; the N90 core ran at an effective 1.80 GHz. 'Each costs roughly one L1 hit' is an assumption about the walker. Safe wording: IF the host process has a 4-level stage 1 with 4 KiB leaves, the host stage 2 has a 40-bit IPA entered at level 1, and every address touched by the walk lies under a 1 GiB stage-2 block, then each stage-2 translation is one descriptor read and a TLB miss needs: 9 references instead of 4 with no caching (+5); 3 instead of 1 with a stage-1 walk cache that yields only the IPA of the last-level table (+2); 2 instead of 1 if that cache holds the PA but there is no IPA->PA cache (+1); no extra read with both (0). The extra reads go to the 8 KiB level-1 stage-2 table (about 32 of its 1024 entries cover RAM on a 32 GiB machine). With 2 MiB or 4 KiB stage-2 leaves the uncached extras are +10 or +15; with a 48-bit IPA (level-0 start) a 1 GiB block already costs +10. Which case applies to FTC862 is unknown. If each extra read cost one L1 hit (4 cycles = 2.22 ns at the N90's effective 1.80 GHz; measured for program loads, assumed for walker reads), +1/+2/+5 reads would be 2.2/4.4/11.1 ns.
